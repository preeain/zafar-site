## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** This is genuinely specific work with four bolted-on generic regions. The composition is not styled, it is *derived*: the Z's diagonal is the same angle in button exit edges, bento corner slices, gallery crops, the hero photo's left edge, and — the best detail on the site — the 4px progress tip on the hero plate, a 4px-deep version of the same cut. Swap the logo for another artist's and the entire ornamental language collapses, which is the correct test. The Punjabi register is load-bearing rather than decorative: `ਜ਼ਫ਼ਰ ਸੰਧੂ` set above the Latin headline, and the Gurmukhi/transliteration lyrics panel, are content no adjacent category could carry.

Where it goes category-interchangeable: the bento ("EVERYTHING, ONE PLACE" with LATEST RELEASE / NEXT SHOW / COMMUNITY / MERCH) is the default 2024-era artist-and-SaaS bento, and its full-width "COMING SOON" merch tile occupies the largest area for the least valuable content; the BTS card rail is a generic horizontal scroller; the three-panel bookings block is the standard industry-contact module; the footer is a generic three-cluster footer. Missed character: the tracklist shows only Latin titles when the release panel proves Gurmukhi typesetting is available, and the progress bar is inert in all three faces when the diagonal was begging to become a scrub handle.

**Deterministic scan.** 45 CLI findings (exit 2), all one rule: `design-system-font-size`, literal `text-[Npx]` values off the documented ramp, across all 12 components. Most of this is noise — the system genuinely uses an 11–13px label band that DESIGN.md documents — but two signals inside it are real: the 10px instances in `components/Hero.tsx:55,73`, independently corroborated by the browser detector's `undersized-ui-text` on "TOP SINGLE", and the one-off 19/22/34px values that sit outside any documented step.

The browser overlay reported 48 itemized findings (its own banner said 29 — the two disagree and are reported as observed). Breakdown: `wide-tracking` ×14, `layout-transition` ×4, `cramped-padding` ×4, `clipped-overflow-container` ×4, `image-hover-transform` ×4, `kicker-above-heading` ×6, `numbered-section-labels` ×6, `tiny-text` ×2, `low-contrast` ×1, `undersized-ui-text` ×1, `em-dash-overuse` ×1, `overused-font` ×1.

**The single most valuable finding came from the detector, and I nearly dismissed it as an artifact.** `overused-font: Primary font: roboto (100% of text)` is literally true. I verified it directly in the page: `--font-archivo` resolves correctly on `<body>` as `"Archivo", "Archivo Fallback"`, but the Tailwind `@theme` tokens `--font-body`, `--font-display`, and `--font-gurmukhi` are **empty at every scope**. `font-family: var(--font-body)` is therefore invalid at computed-value time and silently inherits the browser default stack. Every element on the page — H2s, overlines, Gurmukhi, body — computes to `-apple-system, system-ui, Segoe UI, Roboto, …`. `document.fonts` lists Archivo, Archivo Black and Noto Sans Gurmukhi as `unloaded`.

Assessment A inspected the live page visually and did not catch it, describing the type as reading "like a printed gig poster." That is the detector earning its place: a deterministic scan saw what a design eye rationalized.

## Overall Impression

The design thinking here is genuinely strong — this is one motif carried with real discipline across a long page, and the player family is systems design rather than styling. But it is currently shipping in the wrong typefaces, which means the single most identity-bearing decision in the brand (three fonts, no exceptions) is invisible to every visitor. Fix that one line and the site jumps a full band before anything else is touched.

The biggest opportunity beyond that: the confirmed #1 goal — streaming and presaves — is carried by the page's weakest-weighted elements, while the scarce red is spent on a button that scrolls.

## What's Working

1. **The player family is one idea expressed at three scales.** The identical signal — a red fill whose leading edge carries the diagonal — appears as a 4px strip on the hero plate, a 34px bar in the booth, and an 18px bar in the mini-player. The user learns "red + diagonal tip = live" once and recognizes it everywhere after. First play is the best-designed moment on the site: the plate label flips to red NOW PLAYING, the transport fill goes red, and the mini-player slides up, all in one beat, so the entire playing-state language is taught in a single gesture.

2. **The type system's deliberately empty middle register.** Display at `clamp(36px,5vw,72px)` against 11–13px labels tracked to 0.14–0.24em, with essentially nothing between. Because there is no negotiable middle, every screen resolves in two passes — headline, then labels — and the layout is nearly impossible to get wrong as real content lands. (This is the system that is currently rendering in fallback fonts; the structure survives, the voice doesn't.)

3. **Red rationing makes the CTAs mean something.** Across ~7,900px, red appears roughly six times. On the mobile Circle screen the full-width red JOIN is the only red in view and becomes unmissable, and the playing state reads as *live* rather than *decorated*. The single-red-tile config rule is the right way to protect that.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] None of the three brand fonts are rendering

**What.** The Tailwind v4 `@theme` tokens `--font-body`, `--font-display`, and `--font-gurmukhi` in `app/globals.css:8-10` are empty at every scope, so `body { font-family: var(--font-body) }` (`app/globals.css:22`) is invalid and inherits the browser default. `next/font` is working correctly — `--font-archivo` resolves on `<body>` as `"Archivo", "Archivo Fallback"` — but the theme layer never receives it, because the `.variable` classNames are applied to `<body>` (`app/layout.tsx:54-56`) while `@theme` emits its tokens at `:root`.

**Why it matters.** "Exactly three fonts" is a pinned, non-negotiable brand guardrail and the first Named Rule in DESIGN.md. Archivo Black is the entire display voice; Noto Sans Gurmukhi carries the Punjabi identity that is the most differentiating asset on the page. Right now every visitor sees system sans. Every typographic judgment made in this critique — and every one that will be made in a future polish pass — is being made against type that is not the design.

**Fix.** Move the three `.variable` classNames from `<body>` to `<html>` in `app/layout.tsx` so the custom properties are defined at `:root` where `@theme` resolves them, and/or make the tokens emit unconditionally. Then verify in the browser that `getComputedStyle(document.querySelector('h2')).fontFamily` reports Archivo Black, not `-apple-system`.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P1] Every in-page anchor lands 64px under the sticky nav

**What.** `app/globals.css` sets `scroll-behavior: smooth` but no `scroll-padding-top`, and no section carries `scroll-margin-top`. I verified in-page: `scrollPaddingTop: "auto"`, `#music` `scrollMarginTop: "0px"`. The nav is a 64px sticky bar. All twelve in-page anchors — 6 nav links, LISTEN, LATEST VIDEO, 4 bento tiles, plus the inline "THE CIRCLE" link in `TourSection.tsx:46` — scroll the destination's first 64px behind translucent white.

**Why it matters.** What gets hidden is precisely each section's identity: the `0N — SECTION NAME` overline and the top of the Display H2. The most-used interaction on a long single-page site silently damages the strongest wayfinding device in the system.

**Fix.** One line: `html { scroll-padding-top: 64px; }`.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

**What.** Both assessments measured independently and agree within 0.02. Measured against actual composited backgrounds:

|---|---|---|---|
| `text-ink/55` | every section overline on white | 4.29–4.30 | 4.5 |
| `text-ink/50` | tour column headers, "MORE DATES SOON" | 3.64 | 4.5 |
| `text-white/40` | masters note, privacy line | 3.80–3.81 | 4.5 |
| `placeholder:text-white/38` | EMAIL / CITY | 3.54 | 4.5 |
| `text-ink/90` on cognac | story body copy | 4.11–4.12 | 4.5 |
| `text-white/75` on cognac | story overline | 3.17 | 4.5 |

| `#E03A2F` on ink | NOW PLAYING, NEXT DROP, active track no. | 4.46 | 4.5 |

**Why it matters.** PRODUCT.md records WCAG 2.1 AA as confirmed, and the failing tier is the entire secondary information layer — dates, credits, status labels, privacy reassurance, and the numbered overlines that carry the page's IA. It is also the tier that matters most to a booker skim-reading credits in daylight.

### [P1] The top conversion goal is carried by the page's weakest-weighted elements

**What.** Streaming/presave is the confirmed #1 goal, but the scarcest signal is spent elsewhere. The hero's one red CTA, LISTEN (`Hero.tsx:45-50`), performs an in-page scroll. The actual DSP links (`Hero.tsx:101-113`) are the smallest, faintest things in the hero: 11px, `text-ink/60`, measured **35px tall — below the project's own 44px floor**. PRESAVE appears exactly once, at 13px, in a ledger row ~1,600px down. And the bento tile labelled "STREAMING EVERYWHERE →" has `href: "#music"` (`content/site.ts:74`) — it doesn't go to streaming.

**Why it matters.** A visitor arriving from a social link with eight seconds of intent is offered a scroll button in red and three 11px grey links for the thing the site exists to do. The label/destination mismatch actively spends a click on a scroll.

**Fix.** Make the hero's red CTA do the goal — play immediately, or open a DSP chooser. Promote the three streaming links to ≥44px `.cut-r` outline buttons using the Outline variant already defined in DESIGN.md. Point the release tile at a real DSP link and relabel, or switch `config.redTile` to `'release'` while a next drop is live (one red tile either way).

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable bolder`

### [P1] Persistent chrome cannot be escaped: the mini-player and the mobile menu

**What.** Two instances of one defect class. *Mini-player* (`MiniPlayer.tsx:13-34`): no close control, no next/prev, no seek, no compensating page padding — verified at 390×844 with playback started, the fixed 65px bar completely covers the `© [YEAR] ZAFAR SANDHU` line at the document end, and it is last in tab order despite being permanently on screen. *Mobile menu* (`Nav.tsx:46-77`): no `role="dialog"`/`aria-modal`; Escape does not close it; the background is not inert, so all focusables remain reachable behind the overlay; `body` overflow is never locked; focus is never moved into the overlay on open or restored on close.

**Why it matters.** A user who taps play once pays 65px of an 844px viewport for the rest of the session with no way to opt out, and loses the bottom of the page. A keyboard or screen-reader user who opens the mobile menu is tabbing through content they cannot see, with no escape hatch.

**Fix.** Add a diagonal-cut ✕ to the mini-player mirroring the close button already built at `Nav.tsx:49-55`, and apply `padding-bottom: 72px` to the page while `started` is true. For the menu: `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"`, an Escape handler, `overflow: hidden` on body while open, focus moved to close on open and returned to the hamburger on close. The full-screen menu is also prime real estate on a Persuade surface and currently contains zero conversion actions.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

## Minor Observations

- **Red used outside CTA/live state.** "YOU ARE IN THE CIRCLE." is a confirmation message in Plaid Red (`CircleSection.tsx:50`). Per the One Red Rule that's a defect — white would carry it, or make the moment red by turning it into the next CTA.
- **The YouTube iframe imports everything the system forbids.** `VisualsSection.tsx:16-23` is the largest single element on the page and arrives with rounded chrome, gradient scrims, a second red, and full-saturation colour directly above a deliberately grayscale gallery. A click-to-play façade — poster frame, grayscale until activated, the site's own `.cut-r` play button — fixes the coherence, the extra red, the third-party radius, and the load cost in one move.
- **Placeholder-as-label.** The Circle inputs use EMAIL/CITY as placeholders only; they vanish on first keystroke and measure 3.54:1. The `aria-label`s are correct, so sighted users are the ones penalised. CITY is also never justified — one clause ("so we can tell you when shows land near you") would materially raise completion.
- **Lyrics are bound to the release ledger, not the player.** Play [TRACK TWO], open LYRICS, and you get [DEBUT SINGLE]'s lyrics. Once real lyrics land this reads as a bug.
- **Track durations are authored content, not metadata** (`content/site.ts:37-39`). They match the silent stubs today. When real masters land at different lengths, the tracklist, timecodes, and all three progress fills go wrong silently. Read `loadedmetadata` and treat `dur` as a pre-load fallback.
- **Nav links are 42px tall** — 2px under the stated 44px floor. `py-3` → `py-3.5`.
- **Duplicated logo alt text.** "ZAFAR sandhu" is announced at nav, hero, community, and footer. The nav instance should be `alt=""` inside the already-labelled home link.
- **No skip link**, and the permanently-visible mini-player is last in tab order.
- **Next.js image warnings** on load: `hero-studio.jpg` has `fill` with a 0-height parent, and both the logo and hero photo are detected as LCP without `loading="eager"`.
- **DESIGN.md needs two corrections** from this run: the mobile hero description ("photo on top" — wrong, content precedes photo), and the 10px "TOP SINGLE" exception should be recorded so it stops reading as ramp drift.

## Questions to Consider

1. **What if LISTEN played instead of scrolled?** The one red thing in the hero currently performs a scroll. If tapping it started the track, the mini-player would slide up in the first second of the visit and the visitor would be *in* the experience rather than travelling toward it.
2. **The diagonal cuts everything except the one element the user most wants to touch — why?** The progress fill already carries the Z's tip. Make that tip the scrub handle, and the brand motif stops being decoration and becomes the primary control.
3. **Does the bento earn its 657px?** Strip it and the page loses one genuinely generic module while keeping every destination it links to. What if that space became the presave — a full-bleed room for [NEXT SINGLE] — so goal #1 gets a room, not a ledger row?
4. **What should The Circle's success screen convert into?** Right now the highest-intent moment on the site terminates. "You're in. Now presave [NEXT SINGLE]" would make the peak and the end the same moment.
5. **Why does Gurmukhi appear only twice?** The script is the most differentiating asset on the page. What if track titles carried their Gurmukhi form beneath the Latin one, the way a bilingual record sleeve does?
6. **If a fan only ever sees this page on a phone, with hover disabled — is the design still delivering its best idea?** The colour reward and the gallery's whole payoff are desktop-gated. What is the touch-native version of "colour arrives as a reward for attention"?
